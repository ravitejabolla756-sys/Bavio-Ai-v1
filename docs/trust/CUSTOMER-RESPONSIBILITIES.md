# Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- lawful calling, caller identification, consent, recording notices, opt-outs, DND/DNC rules, and telecommunications requirements;
- truthful AI disclosure and appropriate human handoff;
- rights to uploaded Knowledge, prompts, transcripts, recordings, names, numbers, and other Customer Content;
- Phone Number ownership and provider documentation;
- Agent configuration, industry-specific restrictions, and review of AI output;
- selecting and securing webhook endpoints, API keys, users, and Workspace access;
- validating Leads and ExecutionEvidence before taking business or legal action;
- configuring retention, deletion, export, and privacy-request procedures with Bavio;
- legal and security review for regulated or high-risk use cases.

Customers must not treat a Lead as a customer, AI inference as verified fact, or technical webhook evidence as proof that the external business outcome was correct.
