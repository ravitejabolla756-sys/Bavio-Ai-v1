# Conversations

A Conversation is the persisted call/call-session record and associated transcript or metadata where available. It is not the same as AI Understanding, a Lead, an Action, or a Workflow.

- **Conversation:** call identity, tenant, provider IDs, status, timestamps, duration, transcript/recording references where available.
- **Understanding:** extracted or model-generated interpretation; it can be incomplete or wrong.
- **Activity:** technical events such as action or workflow execution.
- **Evidence:** persisted technical result from an ActionExecution.
- **Outcome:** business result that still requires customer review.

Completion does not guarantee transcript analysis, recording availability, Lead creation, or external delivery.
