# Phone Numbers

Bavio can configure provider phone numbers and call-forwarding/telephony paths where provider inventory and account setup allow. The backend contains Twilio-oriented number provisioning and country eligibility logic.

Number availability, regulatory documents, geographic coverage, porting, emergency calling, caller-ID behavior, and provider continuation vary. Customers must use numbers they own or are authorized to use and must follow the [Telecommunications Terms](../legal/TELECOMMUNICATIONS-TERMS.md).
