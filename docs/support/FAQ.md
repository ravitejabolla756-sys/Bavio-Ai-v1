# Bavio FAQ

## What is Bavio?

Bavio is a business voice-agent platform for configured phone conversations, Knowledge, Leads, technical Actions, and ordered Workflows.

## What is an AI voice Agent?

An Agent uses configured telephony, speech, and language providers to converse. AI output can be incorrect and should be reviewed for consequential uses.

## How do calls reach Bavio?

Through a configured telephony/Phone Number path, currently including Twilio-oriented code. Provider and country availability vary.

## Does Bavio record calls?

Recording depends on the configured path. Customers are responsible for notices and consent; see [Call Recording Consent](../legal/CALL-RECORDING-CONSENT.md).

## How are transcripts used?

Where available, transcripts support Conversation review and structured understanding. They can be incomplete and are not automatically a business conclusion.

## Can Bavio take actions?

Verified actions include Bavio Lead creation and signed webhook delivery. Technical evidence does not prove downstream business success.

## What are Workflows?

The verified workflow is a read-only product surface for an immutable, ordered, sequential Lead-then-webhook flow. It does not document branches or a builder.

## Does Workflow failure undo completed Actions?

No. A successful earlier effect is preserved when a later step fails.
