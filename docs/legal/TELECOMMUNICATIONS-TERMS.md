# Bavio Telecommunications Terms

**STATUS: Draft for legal review**  
**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`

Customers must use Phone Numbers and voice services only for lawful, authorized communications. They are responsible for caller identification, consent, anti-spam and robocalling rules, DND/DNC obligations, opt-outs, number ownership, geographic restrictions, and provider requirements.

Prohibited conduct includes deceptive caller-ID spoofing, unauthorized number use, illegal automated calling, harassment, emergency-service abuse, and attempts to bypass provider controls. Emergency calling availability is not established by the repository and must not be assumed.

Phone Number availability, purchase, porting, geographic coverage, regulatory documentation, and continued provider access may vary. Bavio does not promise availability everywhere. Provider callbacks and network events can fail or arrive late; customers must not represent a call as delivered solely because an Agent was configured.

## Legal review required

Confirm jurisdictions, consent rules, DND/DNC process, emergency limitations, numbering obligations, portability, and customer indemnity.
