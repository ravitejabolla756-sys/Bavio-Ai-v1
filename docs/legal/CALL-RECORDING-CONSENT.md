# Bavio Call Recording and Consent Guidance

**STATUS: Draft for legal review**  
**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`

Recording and transcription laws vary. Some places require notice or consent from one participant; others require notice or consent from all relevant participants. Customers are responsible for determining the rule that applies to each call and for implementing the required notice, consent, opt-out, and retention behavior.

Bavio does not automatically make a recording lawful. Recording depends on the configured telephony path, provider behavior, customer settings, and applicable law. The repository contains recording and transcript storage paths, but does not establish that every call is recorded or that one retention period applies to every path.

Customers should identify the AI voice where required, announce recording where required, stop or avoid recording after a valid opt-out, limit sensitive information, and consult counsel for multi-jurisdiction calling.

## Legal review required

Confirm the actual recording configuration by product path, caller notice wording, consent evidence, opt-out behavior, storage region, retention, and deletion mechanism.
