# Bavio AI and Automation Disclosure

**STATUS: Draft for legal review**  
**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`

Bavio uses AI voice and language components to conduct or assist Conversations, transcribe or structure information, and support business Actions. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a particular decision. It is not a human judgment or professional advice.

Customers must configure truthful disclosure where required, supervise high-risk use, review Agent instructions and Knowledge, and provide human handoff where appropriate. Customers remain responsible for decisions made from AI output and for external effects caused by Actions, Workflows, and webhooks.

Technical ExecutionEvidence confirms that a configured technical action produced a recorded execution result. It does not confirm that the business result, legal basis, caller intent, or AI interpretation was correct.

The customer must not use Bavio to impersonate a person deceptively, clone a real person’s voice without authorization, or make regulated representations beyond its authority.
