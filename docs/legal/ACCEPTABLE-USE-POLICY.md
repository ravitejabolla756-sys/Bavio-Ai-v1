# Bavio Acceptable Use Policy

**STATUS: Draft for legal review**  
**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`

Customers must use Bavio lawfully and with authorization. Prohibited uses include:

- fraud, phishing, credential theft, malware, or social engineering;
- impersonation or deceptive voice cloning without authorization;
- spam, harassment, threats, stalking, illegal robocalling, or evasion of opt-out/DND/DNC requirements;
- deceptive caller-ID spoofing or unauthorized use of Phone Numbers;
- illegal surveillance or unlawful acquisition/scraping of personal data;
- unlawful discrimination or prohibited financial, medical, or legal representations;
- emergency-service abuse or representing an Agent as an emergency service;
- bypassing provider policies, consent requirements, security controls, or usage limits.

Customers must configure truthful Agent disclosure where required, honor opt-outs, protect API credentials, and review high-risk Actions and Workflows. Bavio may investigate, suspend, or terminate abusive use subject to the approved Terms.

Confirm investigation powers, suspension notice, appeals, regulated-sector wording, and safe-harbor language.
