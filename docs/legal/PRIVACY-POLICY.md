# Bavio Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`

This draft describes data flows found in the Bavio repository. It is not a complete jurisdiction-specific notice. Customers must provide any required caller notices and determine their lawful basis.

## 1. Roles

For Workspace and caller data supplied by a customer, the customer generally determines the business purpose and may act as controller/business. Bavio generally operates the service and may act as processor/service provider. The correct role can depend on the deployment and jurisdiction and requires legal review.

## 2. Data categories

- Account and Workspace data: names, email, business profile, onboarding and subscription state, assistant configuration, and authentication metadata.
- Telephony data: Phone Number, caller number, provider call identifiers, call status, timestamps, duration, and routing metadata.
- Conversation data: transcript structures, summaries or analysis where generated, recording references where stored, and conversation metadata.
- AI data: Agent instructions, Knowledge context, prompts, structured extraction, and generated outputs.
- Business execution data: Leads, ActionExecution, ExecutionEvidence, WorkflowExecution, webhook configuration and delivery metadata.
- Technical data: application errors, operational logs, browser storage values, and request metadata where generated by the runtime. The repository does not establish a complete IP/device analytics inventory.
- Billing data: plan, usage, subscription status, processor event identifiers, and payment references. The audited code does not establish storage of full card numbers.

## 3. Purposes

Bavio uses this data to authenticate users, operate voice agents, persist Conversations, produce transcripts or structured understanding, create Bavio Leads, execute configured Actions and Workflows, deliver webhooks, bill usage, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and provide support.

## 4. Storage and providers

The backend uses PostgreSQL/Supabase patterns and Supabase Storage buckets for TTS audio and call recordings where those paths are used. The service can call Twilio, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Cerebras, Groq, OpenAI, Sarvam, Resend, and Dodo paths depending on configuration. Processing locations and subprocessor terms are not fully established and are listed as `[PRIMARY_HOSTING_REGION]` or provider-specific unknowns in [Subprocessors](SUBPROCESSORS.md).

## 5. Customer responsibilities

Customers determine caller notices, recording consent, lawful calling, Knowledge rights, Agent instructions, retention requests, webhook destinations, and access control. See [Call Recording Consent](CALL-RECORDING-CONSENT.md) and [Customer Responsibilities](../trust/CUSTOMER-RESPONSIBILITIES.md).

## 6. Cookies and local storage

The audited frontend uses authentication and onboarding cookies, local storage for tokens and Workspace display state, theme preference, and session storage for country selection. No analytics or advertising cookie integration was established by this audit. Details are in [Cookie Policy](COOKIE-POLICY.md).

## 7. Retention and deletion

The repository contains targeted TTS cleanup paths, including a 24-hour cleanup routine, and call-end TTS cleanup. It does not establish one complete retention schedule or a general self-service deletion flow for all database entities. See [Data Retention](../trust/DATA-RETENTION.md) and [Privacy Requests](../support/PRIVACY-REQUESTS.md).

## 8. Rights and requests

Requests for access, correction, deletion, export, restriction, or objection may be sent to `[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`. Bavio will coordinate with the customer where the customer controls the relevant data. Response periods and identity-verification procedures require legal configuration.

## Legal review required

Confirm controller/processor wording, lawful bases, international transfers, children’s data, rights deadlines, retention, deletion, breach notice, regional notices, and the complete provider list.
