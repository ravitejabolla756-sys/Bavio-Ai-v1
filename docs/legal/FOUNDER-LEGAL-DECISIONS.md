# Founder legal decisions required

**STATUS: Draft for founder and legal review**

Resolve these decisions before publishing the public legal package:

- legal entity name, registered address, incorporation country, and contracting entity;
- effective/update date and approved notice method;
- governing law, venue, dispute process, liability cap, and indemnity language;
- privacy, legal, security, DPA, and support contact addresses;
- controller/processor or business/service-provider role by deployment;
- category-specific retention, deletion, export, backup, and provider-copy behavior;
- recording notice, consent, opt-out, AI disclosure, and human-handoff requirements;
- final plan prices, billing interval, included minutes, usage accounting, top-ups, overages, taxes, credits, proration, and payment processor;
- cancellation timing, refund eligibility, service-failure review, chargebacks, and statutory withdrawal treatment;
- minimum account age and regulated-sector/high-risk use policy;
- approved subprocessors, locations, transfer mechanism, notice, and objection process;
- published API limits, support response targets, service level, maintenance, and incident notification;
- whether the legal index, security overview, trust center, and each policy are ready for public exposure.

Do not replace these with assumptions in customer-facing copy. Record each decision with owner, date, evidence, and counsel approval.
